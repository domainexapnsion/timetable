### 📖 PSM & Clinical Abbreviations Glossary
* **ANC:** Antenatal Care
* **PNC:** Postnatal Care
* **NCD:** Non-Communicable Disease
* **NPNCD:** National Programme for Non-Communicable Diseases
* **IDSP:** Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme
* **HIV / NACP:** Human Immunodeficiency Virus / National AIDS Control Programme
* **NVBDCP:** National Vector Borne Disease Control Programme
* **NPCB:** National Pollution Control Board *(printed in departmental notice)*
* **NTEP:** National Tuberculosis Elimination Programme
* **IMNCI:** Integrated Management of Neonatal and Childhood Illness
* **ESI:** Employees' State Insurance
* **LGT:** Large Group Teaching / Lecture
* **SGT:** Small Group Teaching
* **DOAP:** Demonstration, Observation, Assistance and Performance
* **PSM:** Preventive and Social Medicine
* **MOH:** Medical Officer of Health
